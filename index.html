Research

My public research is on explainable classification of epileptic seizures from EEG. Day to day I also work with CNNs and RNNs for computer vision, and with classical models for language and prediction.

2024

Conference paper · ACIT 2024

Explainable AI for Epileptic Seizure Prediction: A SHAPE and LIME Approach

I am a co-author, with Md. Simul Hasan Talukder, Sohag Kumar Mondal, Mohammad Aljaidi, Rejwan Bin Sulaiman, and Ahmad Al-Qerem. The paper compares gradient boosting, decision trees, random forests, CatBoost, and extra trees on preprocessed EEG recordings. Extra trees is reported at 99.51% accuracy. SHAP and LIME explain how that model reaches a classification.
